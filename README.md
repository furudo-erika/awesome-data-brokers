## Why Data Brokers Matter

Businesses buy third-party data to verify identities, assess risk, enrich CRM records, and reach new customers. Because data brokers handle personal information, choosing a reputable data broker with clear sourcing and compliance is as important as data coverage.

**Privacy note:** If you are an individual looking to remove your information, see the [California Privacy Protection Agency](https://cppa.ca.gov/) data broker registry and the opt-out links on each provider's website. This list is for businesses evaluating compliant data sources, not for locating or tracking individuals.

## Selection Criteria

Every entry in this list of best data brokers was assessed against these criteria:

- Transparent data sourcing and consent practices
- Compliance with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, FCRA, and state data-broker laws
- Accuracy and refresh frequency
- Fit for a specific use case (marketing, B2B, risk, property)
- Clear consumer opt-out processes

## Top 10 Data Brokers Reviewed

### 1. Acxiom

[Acxiom](https://www.acxiom.com/) — Acxiom, part of IPG, is one of the oldest and largest consumer data companies, offering audience data, identity resolution, and data-collaboration services for marketing.

- **Type:** Marketing data & identity
- **Best for:** Enterprise marketers that need consumer data and identity resolution
- **Consider:** Consumer data use is heavily regulated; confirm consent and permitted use.

### 2. Experian

[Experian](https://www.experian.com/business) — Experian provides credit reporting, identity verification, fraud prevention, and marketing audience data across many countries.

- **Type:** Credit bureau + marketing data
- **Best for:** Credit, identity, and marketing data under one provider
- **Consider:** Credit data is governed by FCRA and similar laws; permissible purpose required.

### 3. Equifax

[Equifax](https://www.equifax.com/business/) — Equifax offers consumer and commercial credit data, The Work Number income and employment verification, and analytics for risk decisions.

- **Type:** Credit bureau + data analytics
- **Best for:** Credit risk, income and employment verification, and identity data
- **Consider:** Regulated data; strict access and use requirements.

### 4. TransUnion

[TransUnion](https://www.transunion.com/business) — TransUnion supplies credit data plus identity, fraud, and marketing solutions (including its TruAudience identity graph) to businesses.

- **Type:** Credit bureau + identity
- **Best for:** Risk, identity resolution, and marketing solutions
- **Consider:** As with all bureaus, use is limited by law and contract.

### 5. LiveRamp

[LiveRamp](https://liveramp.com/) — LiveRamp connects first-party data across partners using its RampID identity framework and clean-room technology, rather than selling raw personal data.

- **Type:** Data collaboration & identity
- **Best for:** Privacy-conscious data collaboration and clean rooms
- **Consider:** Value depends on your own first-party data and partner network.

### 6. Epsilon

[Epsilon](https://www.epsilon.com/) — Epsilon, owned by Publicis Groupe, combines consumer data, identity (CORE ID), and ad tech for personalized marketing at scale.

- **Type:** Marketing data & ad tech
- **Best for:** Large brands running people-based marketing and media
- **Consider:** Typically bundled with agency or media services.

### 7. LexisNexis Risk Solutions

[LexisNexis Risk Solutions](https://risk.lexisnexis.com/) — LexisNexis Risk Solutions aggregates public and proprietary records for identity verification, fraud detection, KYC/AML, insurance underwriting, and government use.

- **Type:** Public records & risk data
- **Best for:** Identity verification, fraud, insurance, and compliance
- **Consider:** Access is vetted and use-case restricted.

### 8. ZoomInfo

[ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/) — ZoomInfo is a leading B2B data platform with company, contact, technographic, and intent data for go-to-market teams.

- **Type:** B2B contact & company data
- **Best for:** Sales and marketing teams prospecting into businesses
- **Consider:** B2B contact data still falls under GDPR/CCPA; review compliance terms.

### 9. Dun & Bradstreet

[Dun & Bradstreet](https://www.dnb.com/) — Dun & Bradstreet maintains a global database of business records identified by the D-U-N-S Number, used for commercial credit, supplier risk, compliance, and sales.

- **Type:** Commercial business data
- **Best for:** Company credit, supplier risk, and firmographics
- **Consider:** Business-focused; not a consumer data source.

### 10. CoreLogic

[CoreLogic](https://www.corelogic.com/) — CoreLogic provides property, mortgage, and hazard data and analytics to lenders, insurers, and real estate professionals.

- **Type:** Property data & analytics
- **Best for:** Real estate, mortgage, and insurance property data
- **Consider:** Domain-specific; strongest in US property markets.

## FAQ

### What is a data broker?

A company that collects personal or business information from public records, commercial sources, and partners, then sells or licenses it to other organizations.

### Are data brokers legal?

Yes in most countries, but they are regulated. In the US, credit data falls under FCRA, and states such as California, Vermont, Oregon, and Texas require data broker registration. The EU's GDPR applies to personal data of EU residents.

### How do I remove my data from data brokers?

Use each broker's opt-out page. California residents can use the state's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) to send one deletion request to all registered data brokers.

### Data broker vs data provider?

The terms overlap. "Data provider" is often used for B2B or aggregated data; "data broker" usually refers to companies trading personal consumer data.
